# ExecuAI HR Policy

## 1. Introduction
This HR Policy outlines the standards, expectations, and benefits for all employees at ExecuAI. Our goal is to foster a productive, inclusive, and transparent work environment.

## 2. Recruitment and Onboarding
- ExecuAI is an equal opportunity employer.
- All new hires must undergo a standard onboarding process, including system provisioning and orientation.
- Probation period for new employees is 90 days.

## 3. Leave Management
### 3.1 Leave Types
- **Sick Leave**: 12 days per year.
- **Casual Leave**: 10 days per year.
- **Earned Leave**: 15 days per year (accrued monthly).
- **Maternity/Paternity Leave**: As per statutory requirements.

### 3.2 Approval Process
- All leave requests must be submitted through the ExecuAI Portal.
- Requests should be submitted at least 48 hours in advance (except for emergencies).
- Approval is subject to manager review and department workload.

## 4. Performance and Growth
- Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually.
- We encourage continuous learning and provide a budget for professional certifications and courses.

## 5. Code of Conduct
- Professional behavior is expected at all times.
- ExecuAI has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or bullying.

## 6. Remote Work and Flexibility
- We support a hybrid work model.
- Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

## 7. Termination and Resignation
- Resignation requires a notice period of 30 days.
- ExecuAI reserves the right to terminate employment in cases of gross misconduct or consistent performance failure.
