# ExecuAI Company Policy

## 1. Mission and Vision
**Mission**: To empower enterprises with agentic automation that simplifies complex workflows.
**Vision**: To be the world's most intelligent workforce orchestration platform.

## 2. Workplace Guidelines
### 2.1 Office Etiquette
- Maintain a clean and organized workspace.
- Respect common areas and shared resources.
- Use conference rooms for meetings to minimize noise in open areas.

### 2.2 Communication
- Internal communication should primarily happen via Slack or Email.
- Professionalism is required in all communications, both internal and external.

## 3. Technology and Security
### 3.1 Data Privacy
- Employees must handle sensitive client and company data with extreme care.
- Unauthorized sharing of confidential information is strictly prohibited.

### 3.2 System Access
- Access to enterprise systems (GitHub, AWS, etc.) is granted based on role necessity.
- MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) is mandatory for all core accounts.

### 3.3 Equipment
- Company-provided equipment should be used primarily for work-related tasks.
- Any damage or loss should be reported to the IT team immediately.

## 4. Health and Safety
- ExecuAI is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace.
- Employees should report any safety hazards to the Admin team.

## 5. Expenses and Reimbursement
- All business-related expenses must be pre-approved.
- Receipts must be submitted within 15 days of the expense.

## 6. Ethics and Compliance
- We operate with the highest ethical standards.
- Compliance with local laws and international trade regulations is mandatory.
